## 6.4.2 Star Topology

In a star topology each node in network communicates with the others via a central switch or hub. The hub works as a data flow repeater.

EXAMPLE:
Think of a school principal's office connected to all classrooms through intercoms. The principal's office is the hub, and the classrooms are the nodes.
