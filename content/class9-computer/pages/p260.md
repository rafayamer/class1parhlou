## 12.5.3 Prototyping and Iteration

Prototyping involves creating a preliminary model of your product or service to test and refine your business idea. Iteration is the process of making improvements based on feedback.

**Building Prototypes:** Prototypes can be simple sketches, digital models, or even physical samples. The goal is to visualize and test your ideas early on.

[FIGURE Figure 12.19: Creating a Prototype: a sheet of paper with a teapot sketch and a pencil.]

**Refining Business Ideas:** Gathering feedback from potential customers and stakeholders is crucial. Use this feedback to make necessary adjustments and improve your product or service.

DID YOU KNOW?

Many successful companies, like Facebook and Airbnb, started with simple prototypes and iterated based on user feedback to become the giants they are today.

## 12.6Ethical and Sustainable Entrepreneurship

**Entrepreneurship isn't just about making profits;**

It is also about conducting business in a way that is ethical and sustainable. This section explores the principles of ethical entrepreneurship and how businesses can align their goals with the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).

## 12.6.1 Ethical Practices and Sustainable Growth

Ethical practices in business ensure that a company operates in a way that is fair, transparent, and beneficial to all stakeholders, including customers, employees, and the community. Sustainable growth focuses on developing a business that meets present needs without compromising the ability of future generations to meet their own needs.
