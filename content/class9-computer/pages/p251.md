### Digital Marketing Strategies

• **Search Engine Optimization (SEO):** Optimizing website content to rank higher in search engine results, increasing visibility and traffic.  
• **Social Media Marketing:** Using social media platforms to promote products, engage with customers, and build brand awareness.  
• **Content Marketing:** Creating valuable content to attract and retain customers, such as blogs, videos, and infographics.  
• **Email Marketing:** Sending targeted emails to potential and existing customers to promote products and build relationships.
