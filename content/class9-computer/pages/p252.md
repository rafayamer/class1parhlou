## 12.2.3.2 E-commerce Platforms

E-commerce platforms like Amazon, eBay, and Shopify allow entrepreneurs to set up online stores, manage inventory, process payments, and provide customer service. These platforms provide a convenient way for businesses to sell products to a global audience.

## 12.2.4 Case Studies

### 12.2.4.1 Daraz

Daraz is a leading e-commerce platform in South Asia, including Pakistan. It has transformed the shopping experience by offering a wide range of products, secure payment options, and efficient delivery services.

### 12.2.4.2 Bykea

Bykea is a Pakistani startup that uses mobile apps to provide on-demand transportation and delivery services. It has leveraged digital technology to address transportation challenges in urban areas.
