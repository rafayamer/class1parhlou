## 9.3 Transpiration

The loss of water in the form of vapours from plant surface is called transpiration. This loss may occur through stomata in leaves, through the cuticle present on leaf epidermis, or through special openings called lenticels present in the stems of some plants.

Most of the transpiration occurs through stomata and is called stomatal transpiration. In leaves, water moves from the xylem into the cell walls of mesophyll cells. From the moist walls of mesophyll cells, water evaporates into the air spaces of the leaf. These water vapours then move towards the stomata and then pass to the outside air.
