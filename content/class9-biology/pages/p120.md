## 8.1 Atp: The Cell’S Energy Currency

Cells use a special energy currency for their reactions. This currency is a nucleotide called **adenosine triphosphate (ATP)**. When cells store energy, they make ATP. When cells need energy, they break ATP. ATP molecule has three subunits i.e. **adenine**, (a nitrogen containing base); **ribose** (a five-carbon sugar) and three **phosphate** groups.

In 1941, the Nobel prize winner, Fritz Lipmann proposed that ATP is the main energy-transfer molecule in the cell.
