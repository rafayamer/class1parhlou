### Organ Systems in Human Body

Here are a few examples of organ systems which form the human body.

Skeletal system: It consists of bones, cartilages and tendons. It provides structure, support, and protection to the body. It serves as a framework for muscles to attach, allowing movements. It also stores minerals and produces blood cells.

Digestive System: It consists of organs such as mouth, oesophagus, stomach, small intestine, and large intestine. This system is responsible for the digestion of food and the absorption of digested food.

Respiratory System: It includes the lungs, trachea (windpipe), and bronchi. This system is involved in the exchange of gases (oxygen and carbon dioxide) between the body and the environment.

Muscular System: It includes skeletal muscles, which attach to bones. These muscles contract to move bones. It allows movements and locomotion in the body.
