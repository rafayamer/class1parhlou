Stem cells also remain in different parts of the body throughout life. These stem cells can divide and differentiate into specific cells as the body needs them. They can also regenerate damaged tissue under the right conditions. For example, stem cells present in skin help in wound healing. Stem cells present in liver also help it to repair after damage. Stem cells present in the bone marrow differentiate to make different types of blood cells and immune cells.

In some parts of the body, such as the gut and bone marrow, adult stem cells regularly divide to produce new tissues for maintenance and repair.
