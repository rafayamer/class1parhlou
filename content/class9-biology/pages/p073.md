### 2. Meiosis brings genetic diversity

Crossing over creates new combinations of genes on chromosomes. Each chromosome in the gametes carries a unique set of genes. When diverse gametes from two parents combine, the resulting zygote is genetically different from both parents. In this way, meiosis contributes to genetic diversity in populations.

### Errors in Meiosis

During meiosis-I, chromosomes separate while during meiosis-II sister chromatids separate. It is called disjunction. Sometimes non-disjunction occurs. Due to it, the daughter cells (gametes) receive more or less than the normal number of chromosomes. If such gametes fuse, the zygote receives abnormal number of chromosomes. If such zygote develops, the resulting offspring suffers from severe medical problems.
