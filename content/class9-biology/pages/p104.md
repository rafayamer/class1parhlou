## 6.6 The Working Of Dna And Rna

The DNA molecule in a chromosome consists of thousands of nucleotides. Along the length of DNA molecule, there are specific segments called genes. Each gene consists of specific sequence of nucleotides that carries information for the synthesis of a specific protein.

During the working of a gene, the specific sequence of DNA nucleotides is copied. This copy is in the form of a molecule of messenger RNA (mRNA). The process of making mRNA copy of DNA is called transcription. The mRNA carries the sequence of its nucleotides to the ribosome. The ribosome reads this sequence and joins specific amino acids to form a protein. This step is known as translation.
