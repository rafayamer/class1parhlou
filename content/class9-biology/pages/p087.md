**Nervous System:** It includes brain, spinal cord, and nerves. The nervous system coordinates and controls body functions through nerve impulses.

**Blood Circulatory System:**It includes heart, blood vessels (arteries, veins, and capillaries), and blood. This system transports oxygen, nutrients, hormones, and waste products throughout the body.
