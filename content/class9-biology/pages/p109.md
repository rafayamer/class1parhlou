# Chapter 7 ENZYMES

STUDENT LEARNING OUTCOMES

After studying this chapter, students will be able to:
- Define metabolism, catabolism and anabolism with examples.
- Define enzymes and describe their characteristics.
- Describe and depict the mechanism of enzyme action.
- Describe the factors which could influence enzyme activity.
- Describe competitive and non-competitive enzyme inhibition.

Enzymes are remarkable proteins which speed up biochemical reactions that would otherwise occur at very slow speed. In this chapter, we will study the unique characteristics of enzymes and uncover how they perform their work. We will also explore the various factors that influence their activity.

## 7.1 Metabolism

Metabolism is the sum of all chemical reactions that occur within an organism to sustain life. There are two sub-sets of metabolism i.e., catabolism and anabolism.

1. **Catabolism** involves the breakdown of complex molecules into simpler ones, releasing energy in the process. Examples include:

- Cellular respiration in which food (glucose) molecule is broken into CO₂ and H₂O to get energy.
