### Importance of Homeostasis

Homeostasis plays a vital role in supporting overall health and well-being. For example;

• Each cell is a sophisticated machine that plays a precise role within the body. The proper functioning of cell is possible only when extracellular conditions such as temperature, pH, and the concentrations of salts, glucose and oxygen are kept in normal range.

• When we do hard work, our muscles produce heat that raises the body temperature. In such situation, the temperature regulating centre in the brain sends message to sweat glands. As a result, sweating occurs and the temperature of the body falls to normal.

• When the body temperature falls, the temperature regulatory centre sends message to muscles. As a result, shivering occurs to generate heat.
