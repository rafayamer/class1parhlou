### 1- Light Reactions

During light reaction, light energy is used to make high-energy molecules (ATP and NADPH). Following are the key events of light reactions:

- When chlorophyll absorbs light, two of its electrons become high-energy electrons. These high energy electrons are released from chlorophyll.
- The high energy electrons are passed to an electron transport chain. In this chain, when electrons pass from higher to lower energy level, they release energy which is used to produce ATP.
- Light also breaks water molecule. Oxygen is released while hydrogen atoms give electrons to chlorophyll and become hydrogen ions.
- The electrons of chlorophyll, after the production of ATP, and the hydrogen ions of water are used to reduce a NADP into NADPH (Nicotinamide Adenine Dinucleotide Phosphate - reduced)-.

**Nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide (NAD):**  
It is a coenzyme. One form of this coenzyme also carries phosphate. It is called NADP.
