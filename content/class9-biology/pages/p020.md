### Experiments on Human Beings

In the end, experiments were performed on human beings to test this hypothesis. In 1898, Italian biologists allowed an *Anopheles* mosquito to bite a malarial patient. The infected mosquito was then allowed to bite a healthy man. This person later became ill with malaria. In this way, it was confirmed that mosquitoes transmit *Plasmodium* and so are involved in the spread of malaria.
