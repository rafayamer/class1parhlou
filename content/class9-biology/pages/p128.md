### Use of Respiratory Energy in Body

Respiratory energy (ATP) produced during cellular respiration is used in various ways:

1. Muscle contractions and movement
2. Active transport of substances
3. Synthesis of biomolecules
4. Replication of DNA and mitosis
5. Transmission of nerve impulse
6. Maintenance of body temperature
7. Break down and elimination of toxins from the body.

Table: Difference between aerobic and anaerobic respiration

|  | Aerobic Respiration | Anaerobic Respiration |
|---|---|---|
| Presence of Oxygen | Yes | No |
| Number of ATP as net profit | 36 | 02 |
| Final products | CO₂,H₂O | Lactic acid or Ethanol + CO₂ |
