### 2. Induced Fit Model

This model was proposed by an American biologist Daniel Koshland in 1958. According to this model, the active site of enzyme is not rigid. When substrate interacts with the enzyme, its active site is reshaped to perform its function.

## 7.4 Factors That Affect The Activity Of Enzymes

Enzymes are sensitive to their environment. The activity of an enzyme is affected by the following factors:

### 1. Temperature

Each enzyme works at maximum rate at a specific temperature called optimum temperature. The optimum temperature for most of the human enzymes is 37°C. When temperature rises to a certain limit, the heat adds in the movement of molecules. So, the rate of enzyme action increases.
