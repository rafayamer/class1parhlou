EXAMPLE 2:

Calculate the relative atomic mass of light isotope of chlorine when its relative atomic mass is taken as 35.45.

Relative isotopic abundance of Cl-37 = 24.23%

Relative isotopic abundance of light isotope of chlorine = 75.77%

Relative atomic mass of chlorine = 35.45

Relative atomic mass of chlorine

= (Cl × 75.77 + 37 × 24.23) / 100

35.45 = (Cl × 75.77 + 37 × 24.23) / 100

3545 = Cl × 75.77 + 37 × 24.23

3545 − 896.51 = Cl × 75.77

2648.49 / 75.77 = Cl

34.95 = Cl

Relative atomic mass of light isotope of chlorine is 34.95.
