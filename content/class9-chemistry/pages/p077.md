Let us now examine the reason why the chemical reactions are either exothermic or endothermic.

A chemical reaction mainly involves the processes which involve bond breaking and bond formation. In the following reaction, the chemical bonds between the atoms present in the molecules of H₂ and O₂ first break to give their atoms.

[FIGURE: 2 H₂(g) + O₂(g) → 2 H₂O(g). Below: 2H₂(g) → 4H(g), labelled “Molecule” and “Atoms”; O₂(g) → 2O(g), labelled “Molecule” and “Atoms”.]

These atoms of hydrogen then form bonds with oxygen atoms to form two molecules of gaseous H₂O.

[FIGURE: 4 H(g) + 2 O(g) → two H₂O structures, each showing an O with dots and two H atoms bonded to it.]

Breaking of bonds of H₂ and O₂ absorb energy (endothermic process) while making of bonds between H and O evolve energy (exothermic process). In this reaction, weaker bonds are broken i.e of H₂. Hence less energy is absorbed in the system. While the bonds which are formed in water molecule are stronger and thus greater energy is evolved. Hence, the energy which is evolved is more than the energy which is absorbed. The overall reaction is thus exothermic.
