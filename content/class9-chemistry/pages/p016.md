ACTIVITY

Take 100 g of water in a beaker and prepare saturated solution of sugar at room temperature. Heat the beaker on a spirit lamp. Add a little more sugar in it and stir it. Will this sugar be dissolved in it? You will notice that by heating the solution the quantity of sugar dissolved in water has increased i.e. the solubility of sugar has increased.
