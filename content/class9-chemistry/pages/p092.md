### Effect of the Presence of a Catalyst on the Reversible Reaction

At the stage of dynamic equilibrium, the rates of both forward and backward reactions are equal.

A catalyst increases both the rates of forward and back reactions of a reversible reaction. So if a reversible reaction is carried out in the presence of a catalyst it will decrease the time taken by the reaction to attain the state of equilibrium.

INTERESTING INFORMATION:

Industrial production of ammonia in Haber Process is a very useful application of the phenomenon of chemical equilibrium. Ammonia gas leads to the formation of an important fertilizer urea. The ability of ammonia gas to be converted into its liquid form easily is used to drive the reaction to completion. In this way, practically 100% conversion of N₂ and H₂ to NH₃ is achieved.
