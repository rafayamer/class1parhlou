## 10.5 Strategies to Reduce Environmental Issues

Huge amount of pollutant gases are thrown out in the atmosphere by burning fossil fuels. Automobiles, aeroplanes, industrial machines and coal-fired electricity generating plants, etc. are mainly responsible for the extremely polluted air especially in big cities. Scientists have developed a number of different ways to control this menace of pollution.

Planting trees is thought to be very helpful in removing the air pollution. A well-known process carried out by plants is photosynthesis in which plants clean the air through absorption of carbon dioxide and releasing oxygen. This famous reaction takes place in the presence of sunlight and it is catalysed by chlorophyll, the green pigments present in leaves.
