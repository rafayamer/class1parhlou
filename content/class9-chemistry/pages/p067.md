EXAMPLE 3:
Aluminium metal reacts with oxygen to produce aluminium oxide. How many grams of oxygen will be required to react completely with 0.3 moles of aluminium?

SOLUTION:
Moles of Al = 0.3    Grams of O₂ used = ?

The balanced chemical equation for the reaction.

4Al (s) + 3O₂ (g) → 2Al₂O₃ (s)

According to this equation

4 moles of aluminium need oxygen = 3.0 moles  
1.0 mole of aluminium will need oxygen = 3/4  
0.3 moles of aluminium will need oxygen = 3/4 × 0.3  
= 0.225 moles  
1 mole of oxygen (O₂) has molar mass = 32 g  
0.225 mole of oxygen (O₂) will have molar mass = 32 × 0.225  
= 7.2 g

EXAMPLE 4:
How many molecules of water will be produced if we react 5 g of hydrogen gas with excess of oxygen gas?

SOLUTION:
Mass of H₂ used = 5g    Molecules of water produced = ?

The balanced chemical equation for the reaction:

2H₂(g) + O₂(g) → 2H₂O(l)  
2 moles  1 mole  2 moles  
4g    32 g   36 g

According to this equation

4 g of hydrogen produce H₂O = 36 g of H₂O  
5 g of hydrogen will produce H₂O = 36/4 × 5 = 45g of H₂O  
18g (1 mole) of H₂O contain molecules = 6.022 × 10²³  
36g of H₂O contain molecules = 6.022 × 10²³ × 2  
= 12.04 × 10²³  
45g of H₂O contain = 45/36 × 12.04 × 10²³  
= 1.505 × 10²⁴ molecules
