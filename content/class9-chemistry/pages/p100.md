Water has an essential role to play in Arrhenius concept of acids and bases. Whenever an acid or a base dissociates in water, its molecules participate in reaction by surrounding the resultant proton (H⁺) and hydroxyl ion(OH⁻). Since proton is very small in size and its charge density is very high, it forms a strong bond with the lone pair of water molecule to give hydroxonium ion, H₃O⁺.

H₂O + H⁺ ⇌ [H₃O]⁺

According to some reports upto four water molecules may surround the proton. Similarly, hydroxyl ions are also surrounded by water molecules as shown in the Figure (7.1).

Arrhenius also explained the process of neutralization. According to him when a strong acid and a strong base are dissolved in water, they completely dissociate into ions.
