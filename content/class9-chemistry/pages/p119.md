### Density

It is mass of a substance in a unit volume. Density of elements generally increases from top to bottom in a group but varies less significantly from left to right in a period. It is expressed in g/cm³.

Table (8.11) Densities of First Group Elements

|  | Li | Na | K | Rb | Cs |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| First group Elements density | 0.53 | 0.97 | 0.89 | 1.63 | 1.879 |
