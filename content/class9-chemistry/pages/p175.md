## 13.2 Hazard Signs

A chemistry laboratory is a strict area where rigorous rules must be practised to avoid a chance of a deadly accident. A dangerous situation may arise not only for the individuals working there but for the whole area. In a laboratory there are several hazardous materials, sensitive equipments and specified areas for specific tasks. Proper warning signs ought to be posted on these areas to ensure that every person entering there must understand and act accordingly to maintain laboratory safety.

Several signs and symbols are posted in different areas of the lab. and bottles containing hazardous chemicals. These signs indicate that specific precautions must be observed according to the requirement of the sign posted there. If you see such signs, you must be alert and take extra care to maintain safety in that area, Fig (13.1).
