### Formation of Covalent Compounds

### Water

A water molecule is formed when two hydrogen atoms share their electrons separately with the electrons of one oxygen atom.

[FIGURE: An oxygen atom, labelled O and surrounded by electron dots, plus two hydrogen atoms, each labelled H, with red crosses for electrons, leads by an arrow to two diagrams of a water molecule. Each shows O with two H atoms and shared dot-and-cross electron pairs. Two electron-dot representations below show O bonded to two H atoms, with lone pairs on O.]

### Carbon dioxide

A carbon dioxide molecule is formed when an atom of carbon shares its four electrons with two oxygen atoms. Each oxygen atom also shares two electrons.

[FIGURE: Two oxygen atoms labelled O and a carbon atom labelled C, each shown with electron dots and crosses, are joined by plus signs and an arrow to a diagram of O, C and O with shared electron pairs. Below are electron-dot and structural representations labelled O, C and O, showing O=C=O.]
