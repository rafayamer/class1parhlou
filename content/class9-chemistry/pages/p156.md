## 2. Combustion

Alkanes burn in oxygen or air to form CO₂ and H₂O with the evolution of
large amount of heat. The reaction is called combustion.

CH₄ (g) + 2O₂ (g) → CO₂ (g) + 2H₂O (g) + heat  
Methane

2CH₃ — CH₃ (g) + 7O₂ (g) → 4CO₂ (g) + 6H₂O (g) + heat  
Ethane

INTERESTING INFORMATION:

A mixture of natural gas (methane) and air may explode when ignited. This is
the main cause of explosion at homes where gas leakage occurs.
