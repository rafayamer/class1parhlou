Q3.8. A ball of mass 0.4 kg is dropped on the floor from a height of 1.8 m. The ball rebounds straight upward to a height of 0.8 m. What is the magnitude and direction of the impulse applied to the ball by the floor?

ANSWER: (4 N s, upward)

Q3.9. Two balls of masses 0.2 kg and 0.4 kg are moving towards each other with velocities 20 m s⁻¹ and 5 m s⁻¹ respectively. After collision, the velocity of 0.2 kg ball becomes 6 m s⁻¹. What will be the velocity of 0.4 kg ball?

ANSWER: (2 m s⁻¹)
