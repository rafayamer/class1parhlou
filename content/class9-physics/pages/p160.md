SECTION E: NUMERICAL PROBLEMS

Q7.1. The temperature of a normal human body on Fahrenheit scale is 98.6°F. Convert it into Celsius scale and Kelvin scale.

ANSWER: (37°C, 310 K)

Q7.2. At what temperature Celsius and Fahrenheit thermometer reading would be the same?

ANSWER: (- 40°)

Q7.3. Convert 5°F to Celsius and Kelvin scale.

ANSWER: (-15°C, 258 K)

Q7.4. What is equivalent temperature of 25°C on Fahrenheit and Kelvin scales?

ANSWER: (77°F, 298 K)

Q7.5. The ice and steam points on an ungraduated thermometer are found to be 192 mm apart. What temperature will be on Celsius scale if the length of mercury thread is at 67.2 mm above the ice point mark?

ANSWER: (35°C)

Q7.6. The length between the fixed point of liquid-in-glass thermometer is 20 cm. If the mercury level is 4.5 cm above the lower mark, what is the temperature on the Fahrenheit scale?

ANSWER: (72.5°F)
