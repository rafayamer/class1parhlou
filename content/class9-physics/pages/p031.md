### Resultant Vector

We can add two or more vectors to get a single vector. This is called as resultant vector. It has the same effect as the combined effect of all the vectors to be added. We have to determine both magnitude and direction of the resultant vector, therefore, it is quite different from that of scalar addition. One method of addition of vectors is the graphical method.
