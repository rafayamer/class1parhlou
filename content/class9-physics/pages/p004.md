# About the Author

Professor (Rtd) Muhammad Nisar is an esteemed and experienced author, holds M.Sc. degree from University of the Punjab. He also attended a training program in Leeds University and Sheffield University UK under Columbo plan. He was a member of revision of Curricula, Textbooks and Teaching Aids Committee, constituted by the Government of the Punjab in 1999. He was awarded Governor’s Education Gold Medal for developing National Curricula in General Science and Physics in 2001. He has been co-author and editor of more than 20 textbooks in Physics from class 9 – 12 and General Science from class 4 – 10 published by Punjab Textbook Board. He also developed Science Lesson Plans and Teacher’s Guides at Directorate of Staff Development Punjab. He is also the author of Outsourced Science and General knowledge textbooks based on National Curriculum 2006. He has reviewed a number of Science and Physics textbooks and SRMs for PCTB. His reading material is a valuable resource for students and teachers.
