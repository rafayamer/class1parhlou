7. **Relativistic Mechanics:** It explains how space and time are not absolute quantities but related to observer. It describes the relationship between them and how they are influenced by gravity and speed.

[FIGURE: Earth and a small object on a curved grid; caption: Einstein view of gravity as space time curvature]

[FIGURE: An atom with a red central nucleus and three orbiting particles; caption: Nuclear atom]

9. **Particle Physics:** It is the study of subatomic particles and elementary particles which are basic building blocks of matter.

[FIGURE: A circular diagram with three particles labelled U, d and d, connected by a wavy line; caption: Quark Structure of a Neutron]
