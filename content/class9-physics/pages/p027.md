SECTION E: NUMERICAL PROBLEMS

Q1.1. Calculate the number of seconds in a (a) day (b) week (c) month and state your answers using SI prefixes.

ANSWER: (86.4 ks, 604.8 ks, 2.592 Ms)

Q1.2. State the answers of problem 1.1 in scientific notation.

ANSWER: [8.64 × 10⁴ s, 6.048 × 10⁵ s, 2.592 × 10⁶ s]

Q1.3. Solve the following addition or subtraction. State your answers in scientific notation.

(a) 4 × 10⁻⁴ kg + 3 × 10⁻⁵ kg  (b) 5.4 × 10⁻⁶ m − 3.2 × 10⁻⁵ m

ANSWER: [(a) 4.3 × 10⁻⁴ kg (b) − 2.66 × 10⁻⁵ m]

Q1.4. Solve the following multiplication or division. State your answers in scientific notation.

(a) (5 × 10⁴ m) × (3 × 10⁻² m)  (b) (6 × 10⁸ kg)/(3 × 10⁴ m³)

ANSWER: (a) 1.5 × 10³ m²  (b) 2.0 × 10⁴ kg m⁻³

Q1.5. Calculate the following and state your answer in scientific notation.

((3 × 10² kg) × (4.0 km))/(5 × 10² s²)

ANSWER: (2.4 × 10³ kg m s⁻²)

Q1.6. State the number of significant digits in each measurement.

(a) 0.0045 m (b) 2.047 m (c) 3.40 m (d) 3.420 × 10⁴ m

ANSWER: [(a) 2 (b) 4 (c) 3 (d) 4]

Q1.7. Write in scientific notation:

(a) 0.0035 m  (b) 206.4 × 10² m

ANSWER: [(a) 3.5 × 10⁻³ m, (b) 2.064 × 10⁴ m)]

Q1.8. Write using correct prefixes:

(a) 5.0 × 10⁴ cm  (b) 580 × 10² g  (c) 45 × 10⁻⁴ s

ANSWER: [(0.5 km, 58 kg, 4.5 ms)]

Q1.9. Light year is a unit of distance used in Astronomy. It is the distance covered by light in one year. Taking the speed of light as 3.0 × 10⁸ m s⁻¹, calculate the distance.

ANSWER: (9.46 × 10¹⁵ m)

Q1.10. Express the density of mercury given as 13.6 g cm⁻³ in kg m⁻³.

ANSWER: (1.36 × 10⁴ kg m⁻³)
