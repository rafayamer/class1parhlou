Q5.7. A girl is swinging on a swing. At the lowest point of her swing, she is 1.2 m from the ground, and at the highest point she is 2.0 m from the ground. What is her maximum velocity and where?

ANSWER: (4 m s⁻¹, at the lowest position)

Q5.8. A person pushes a lawn mower with a force of 50 N making an angle of 45° with the horizontal. If the mower is moved through a distance of 20 m, how much work is done?

[FIGURE: A person pushing a lawn mower outdoors.]

ANSWER: (707 J)

Q5.9. Calculate the work done in

(i) Pushing a 5 kg box up a frictionless inclined plane 10 m long that makes an angle of 30° with the horizontal.

[FIGURE: A box on an inclined plane labelled 10 m and 30°, with the vertical height labelled h.]

ANSWER: (250 J)

(ii) Lifting the box vertically up from the ground to the top of the inclined plane.

ANSWER: (250 J)

Q5.10. A box of mass 10 kg is pushed up along a ramp 15 m long with a force of 80 N. If the box rises up a height of 5 m, what is the efficiency of the system?

ANSWER: (41.7%)

Q5.11. A force of 600 N acts on a box to push it 5 m in 15 s. Calculate the power.

ANSWER: (200 W)

Q5.12. A 40 kg boy runs up-stair 10 m high in 8 s. What power he developed.

ANSWER: (500 W)

Q5.13. A force F acts through a distance L on a body. The force is then increased to 2F that further acts through 2L. Sketch a force-displacement graph and calculate the total work done.

ANSWER: (5FL or 5 units)
