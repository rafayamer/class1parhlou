Q4.4. A see-saw is balanced with two children sitting near either end. Child A weighs 30 kg and sits 2 metres away from the pivot, while child B weighs 40 kg and sits 1.5 metres from the pivot. Calculate the total moment on each side and determine if the sea-saw is in equilibrium.

ANSWER: (60 N)

Q4.5. A crowbar is used to lift a box as shown in the given figure. If the downward force of 250 N is applied at the end of the bar, how much weight does the other end bear? The crowbar itself has negligible weight.

[FIGURE: A box rests at the left end of a crowbar angled upward to the right. The bar is supported near the box; the distances shown are 5 cm from the box to the support and 30 cm from the support to the downward force F at the right end.]

ANSWER: (1500 N)

Q4.6. A 30 cm long spanner is used to open the nut of a car. If the torque required for it is 150 N m, how much force F should be applied on the spanner as shown in the figure given below.

[FIGURE: A horizontal spanner with its head at the left and a nut at the right; a vertical force F is applied at the right end, with a horizontal dashed line extending to the right.]

ANSWER: (500 N)

Q4.7. A 5 N ball hanging from a rope is pulled to the right by a horizontal force F. The rope makes an angle of 60° with the ceiling, as shown in the given figure. Determine the magnitude of force F and tension T in the string.

[FIGURE: A ball hangs from a slanting rope attached to a horizontal ceiling. The angle between the rope and ceiling is 60°. The rope is labelled T, and a horizontal arrow pointing right toward the ball is labelled F.]

ANSWER: (2.9 N, 5.8 N)

Q4.8. A signboard is suspended by means of two steel wires as shown in the given figure. If the weight of the board is 200 N, what is the tension in the strings?

[FIGURE: A signboard is suspended by two vertical wires labelled T_1 and T_2. The distances from the centre to the wires are marked 2 m on each side. The board is labelled “Signboard”, with a downward arrow labelled 200 N.]

ANSWER: (100 N, 100 N)

Q4.9. One girl of 30 kg mass sits 1.6 m from the axis of a see-saw. Another girl of mass 40 kg wants to sit on the other side, so that the see-saw may remain in equilibrium. How far away from the axis, the other girl may sit?

ANSWER: (1.2 m)

Q4.10. Find the tension in each string as shown in the given figure, if the block weighs 150 N.

[FIGURE: A joint is connected to a horizontal string at the right and a slanting string to a horizontal support above. The angle between the slanting string and the horizontal support is 60°. A block hangs below the joint and is labelled 150 N.]

ANSWER: (86.6 N, 173.2 N)
