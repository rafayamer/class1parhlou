Q4.1. A force of 200 N is acting on a cart at an angle of 30° with the horizontal direction. Find the x and y-components of the force.

ANSWER: (173.2 N, 100 N)

Q4.2. A force of 300 N is applied perpendicularly at the knob of a door to open it as shown in the given figure. If the knob is 1.2 m away from the hinge, what is the torque applied? Is it positive or negative torque?

[FIGURE: A door with hinges along its left edge, a knob on the right edge, a dashed line from the hinge to the knob labelled 1.2 m, and a force arrow labelled F.]

ANSWER: (360 N m, positive)

Q4.3. Two weights are hanging from a metre rule at the positions as shown in the given figure. If the metre rule is balanced at its centre of gravity (C. G), find the unknown weight w.

[FIGURE: A metre rule balanced on a support labelled C.G, with a weight w hanging 40 cm to the left and a 4 N weight hanging 30 cm to the right.]

ANSWER: (3 N)
