| D-029 | 2026-11-16 | **Examiner's analysis after every paper.** The app computes every number (marks by unit, topic and question type; marks lost to missing steps, wrong final answers, wording, unattempted required parts, wrong or blank MCQs; strong ≥75%, needs work 50–74%, weak <50%, judged only with at least 2 marks of evidence). The AI examiner writes the report from those facts in a serious but friendly tutor's voice; it may not change a number, and any weak topic it names must be one of the paper's real topics. Offline, the app writes the report itself from the same numbers. | Founder: "Examiner should tell weak topics and give analysis … complete analysis like tutor with serious tone but still friendly". |
| D-028 | 2026-11-16 | **Papers on paper, marked like the board.** (1) Every paper prints as a board-style PDF with Parhlou branding: roll number boxes, Paper I objective (own time, bubble sheet) and subjective (board numbering "Q2 (iii)", "Q5 (a)/(b)", "attempt any …" notes), paper code, optional marking scheme. (2) "Make my paper": the AI chooses a full 2027-pattern paper from a shortlist the app builds per slot (most repeated board questions, the student's weak topics, unseen questions); the pick is used only if every slot gets its exact count of its own candidates; offline, the app's shortlist is used. (3) Scripts written on paper are scanned in any order; the AI reads each page, splits it by the student's own labels ("2 iii)", "5 b)"), follows answers across pages, ignores struck-through and rough work, and reads the bubble sheet (two circles or a cut = zero, as in the board). The student confirms what was read and places any unlabelled piece. (4) Every answer on paper is double-marked from the page photos: two independent examiners, and a head examiner for any step they disagree on; a step still unsettled is shown as "needs a look", never guessed. Step marks follow the rubric with error carried forward, no method marks for a bare answer, cancelled work not marked. Extra attempts: the first ones in question order count. | Founder: "questions should be picked by ai and made into pdf … board style … can be printed", "ai invigilator should check those hand writing … 100 percent correctly … students write 2b) answer … simulate real marking scheme like board examiners". No AI can promise 100%: double marking, the head examiner, the student's check of the reading and "needs a look" are how errors are caught. BISE's internal examiner instructions are not public; the rules used are those of published step-marking schemes. |
