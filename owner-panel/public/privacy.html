Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 November 2026. Parhlou is a study app for Class 9 students in Punjab, Pakistan. Most of our students are under 18, so we collect as little as we can and never sell data.

1. Who we are

Parhlou is run from Pakistan by its founder. For anything about your data, use Profile → Send feedback in the app, or the contact email on this page once listed. We reply within 7 days.

2. What we collect

  • Account: your email address, the name you type, and (only if you add it) a phone number.
  • Study details: board, class, medium, subjects, study days and favourite subject.
  • Learning records: lessons finished, quiz answers, paper marks, streaks and XP.
  • Ask Ustaad: your questions and Ustaad's answers (kept 30 days), and a short summary of how you like to learn.
  • Photos: pictures of questions or answer sheets you choose to send, used only to answer or mark them.
  • Leagues and friends: your first name, avatar emoji, friend code, weekly XP and streak.
  • Payments (Plus): the wallet type, the transaction ID, the amount and, if you type it, the number you paid from. We never see your PIN, card or wallet balance.
  • Technical: app version and a random install number used to stop abuse of the AI service.

We do not collect your location, contacts, CNIC/B-Form number, or the phone's advertising ID.

3. Why we use it

  • To teach you, plan your study, mark your work and show your progress.
  • To keep you safe: Ustaad only helps with studies, and messages about harm, sexual content or illegal acts are refused and flagged for our safety team.
  • To run Plus payments and keep the records the law requires.
  • To fix problems and improve lessons, using records without names wherever possible.

4. Who can see it

  • You, and (when linking arrives) a parent or teacher you link.
  • Parhlou's team, only to keep students safe, answer support requests and check payments. Every look at a student's account is recorded.
  • Other students see only your first name, avatar, weekly XP and streak, and only in leagues, among friends you added, or in class groups you joined. There is no chat between students. Marks are never shown to others.
  • Service providers who process data for us under contract: Supabase (accounts and database), OpenAI (the AI behind Ustaad and marking; it does not use your data to train its models), Google AdMob (ads on the free plan, set to child-directed: no personalised ads, no advertising ID), and GitHub (app updates). Their servers may be outside Pakistan; data travels encrypted.

We never sell your data and never use it for personalised advertising.

5. Children and parents

Parhlou is made for students aged about 13 to 16. A parent or guardian should help a child set up the app and must approve any payment. Parents can ask us to see, correct or delete their child's data at any time.

6. How long we keep it

  • Ask Ustaad chats: 30 days.
  • Answer-sheet photos: only while marking.
  • Learning records: until you delete your account.
  • Payment records: kept for the period tax law requires, without the link to your account once it is deleted.

7. Your rights

  • See and download your data: Profile → Download my data.
  • Correct it: Profile → Edit profile.
  • Delete your account and data: Profile → Delete my account. This is immediate and cannot be undone.
  • Complain: use Send feedback. If you're not satisfied, you may complain to the relevant Pakistani authority.

8. Security

All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). Sign-in uses one-time codes; the saved login on your phone is encrypted with the phone's hardware key store. In our database, each student can reach only their own records. AI spending and code guessing are limited, and every staff action is logged. No system is perfect: if a breach affects you, we will tell you and the relevant authorities promptly.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, the app shows the new version before it applies.
