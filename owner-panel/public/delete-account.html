Delete your Parhlou account

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open Parhlou and go to Profile.
  2. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

Your account, profile, progress, marks, Ask Ustaad chats and memory, friends, league entries and class-group memberships are deleted straight away and can't be recovered.

Without the app

A student or parent can ask us to delete an account by writing from the account's email address through the contact address on our privacy policy. We delete it within 7 days and confirm by email.

What we keep

Records of Plus payments (amount, date, wallet type and transaction ID) are kept for the period tax law requires, with the link to the student removed. Nothing else is kept.
