Refund Policy

Last updated 19 November 2026.

  • Changed your mind: ask within 7 days of Plus starting and we refund the full amount, to the same JazzCash or Easypaisa number, if you have used less than a quarter of that month's Plus AI allowance.

Ask through Profile → Send feedback with your transaction ID. Refunds are sent within 7 working days of approval.
